HOW TO UNDERSTAND ARABIC

Don't just memorize.
Understand.

Arabic becomes much easier when you begin to see the connections between words. Instead of memorizing every word separately, learn roots, patterns and grammar together.

01 · ROOTS

Find the root.

ك ـ ت ـ ب

The root ك ت ب is connected with writing. Many Arabic words grow from the same root.

02 · PATTERNS

Recognize the pattern.

كَتَبَ → يَكْتُبُ

The pattern changes grammatical meaning while the root carries the central idea.

03 · WORD FAMILY

Build the family.

كَتَبَ · كِتَاب · كَاتِب

One root can create verbs, nouns and related words. This makes vocabulary easier to remember.

04 · GENDER

Look for gender.

طَالِب → طَالِبَة

Arabic nouns and adjectives can be masculine or feminine. The ending ـة often marks feminine.

05 · DEFINITE

Understand ال.

كِتَاب → الْكِتَاب

الـ is the definite article, similar to "the" in English.

06 · PRONOUNS

Watch the endings.

كِتَابِي

Arabic can attach pronouns directly to words. كِتَابِي means "my book."
